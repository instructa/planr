title: Reviews and Approvals
description: Distinguish evidence-based checker work from explicit human decision gates.
---

## What they are

A **ReviewGate** is durable audit state attached to a FeatureRun outcome or plan. It records independent leases, immutable attempts, findings, and acceptance. An **approval** is an explicit human decision attached to an item.

## Why both exist

Technical correctness and authorization are different questions. A reviewer can prove the implementation matches acceptance criteria; only the designated human can authorize a release, cost, deletion, or external side effect.

## Who owns them

The maker submits evidence with plain `planr done`; Planr materiality decides whether a ReviewGate is required. A checker leases the gate with a different `PLANR_WORKER_ID`. The named human approves or denies an approval request. FeatureRun and map state enforce their respective gates.

## How they change

```bash
planr done <item-id> --summary "Implemented feature" --cmd "cargo test" --json
planr pick --plan <plan-id> --work-type review --json
planr review evidence <target-item-id> --json
planr review close <review-gate-id> --verdict complete --json
```

Planr requires the leased reviewer to differ from the responsible maker. A `not-complete` verdict appends an immutable attempt and durable findings to the same gate. The maker resolves named findings, and that gate returns to pending independent re-review; no review or fix map items are created.

```bash
planr approval request <item-id> --reason "production release"
planr approval list --open --json
planr approval approve <item-id> --by "release owner" --comment "window confirmed"
```

Requested or denied approval blocks closure. Inspect the gate with `planr map preview --close <item-id>`.
