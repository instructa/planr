---
title: Evidence and Context
description: Store proof, decisions, discoveries, and artifacts on the right durable surface.
---

## What they are

- **Logs** prove progress, completion, verification, review, or handoff with summaries, files, commands, tests, and findings.
- **Artifacts** point to durable files such as screenshots, reports, or review documents.
- **Context** preserves reusable discoveries, decisions, constraints, risks, blockers, and goal contracts.
- **Notes** are short task-local reminders.

## Why they exist

The next worker needs evidence, not “tests passed” in a vanished chat. Separating proof from reusable knowledge also keeps the map's state authority clear.

## Who owns them

The worker who performed a command records its evidence. Reviewers record findings and verdict evidence. Product or technical owners record decisions and constraints that later work must recall.

## How they change

```bash
planr log add --item <item-id> \
  --summary "Hardened parser boundaries" \
  --files src/parser.rs,tests/parser.rs \
  --cmd "cargo test parser" \
  --tests "parser suite: passed"

planr context add "External IDs remain opaque strings" --item <item-id> --tag decision
planr context list --item <item-id> --json
```

For binding Evidence, use `planr evidence readiness`, the configured run/capture method, and canonical coverage. A `--kind verification` log is narrative and supports frozen pre-Evidence compatibility only; it cannot replace a trusted receipt, review, or approval. Record exact commands and meaningful outcomes, and do not attach unrelated dirty files as proof.
